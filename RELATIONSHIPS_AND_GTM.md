## 3. The pilot acquisition plan

**Frame the entire pilot as "automated process monitoring + audit traceability," never as "AI judging welders."** (Marc validates this language.)

### The offer (low-risk by design)
> "We instrument one station for 30 days, free. You get **audit-ready digital weld logs** for your class submission — keep them regardless of outcome. In shadow mode, your QC makes every real call; our system only watches. After 30 days we show you which patterns predict your rejects."

### What we put in front of them (no model, no labels needed to start)
1. **Audit-ready digital weld log** — every session auto-generates a timestamped PDF (V/I/speed traces, the 11 features, pass/fail flag, welder ID, joint ID) that drops straight into their DNV/Lloyd's submission folder. Solves a *today* pain (paper records in a filing cabinet). Build: ~1–2 weeks on our existing stack.
2. **Welder consistency comparison** — overlay two sessions / two welders on the same joint. Pure visualisation, no labels. Makes invisible craft visible to a superintendent.
3. **WPS-anchored drift alert** — flag deviation from the **approved Welding Procedure Specification envelope** (not the welder's personal average — that produces false alarms and looks naive). Maps to something QC already enforces. Rule-based, fully explainable.

### The real payload underneath the demos
The three demos are the *wrapper*; the **product is label capture**:
- **NDT/RT ingestion** — the yard already shoots radiography/ultrasonic on sampled production welds for class compliance. Tie each NDT report to a session ID and we get **non-destructive ground truth on real production welds** — including the sub-surface defects (LOF, porosity, cracks) our feature model is blind to. *This is the single highest-value, lowest-friction label source and it already exists in their workflow.*
- **One-tap inspector verdict capture** — frictionless logging of verdict + defect type + location, tied to session ID. Without this, shadow mode gives us "reject" with no "why," which is nearly useless for training.
- **Validate on continuous QC measurements, not binary rejects** — reject rates run 1–5%; one station/30 days yields maybe 5–20 rejects, far too few for meaningful precision/recall. Instead validate against the inspector's *continuous* readings (penetration depth, undercut, reinforcement) which exist on **every** weld — turning ~12 datapoints into ~400 and the problem from rare-event classification into regression.

### Non-negotiable: the data clause goes in upfront
Put data rights **in the signed pilot doc at week 0**, framed benignly ("we retain anonymised process data to improve the monitoring service"). Which *algorithm* we run internally is nobody's business — keep that quiet. But *retaining their data* must be explicit and signed. Concealing the data use is exactly what detonates a conservative-yard relationship when legal finds it later.

### Pilot success = the metric that triggers Motional
Define it upfront with the yard: e.g. *"our flags agree with your QC/NDT rejects at >X% precision, validated on continuous QC readings across N sessions."* That number is the Series A trigger.

## 4. Tech stack & why we're the people to build this

### What's already shipped (proof of execution, not a deck)
- **Edge hardware:** ESP32-based sensor rig recording real weld sessions (V/I/speed/heat-dissipation) — working, not a render.
- **Ingestion + backend:** FastAPI service, session database, threshold-based safety/verdict system.
- **Feature layer:** 11 engineered weld-quality features feeding a pass/fail verdict.
- **Reporting:** auto-generated **WelderReportPDF** (cost summary, rejection summary, findings, corrective actions) — i.e. the audit-log demo is already most of the way built.
- **Demo surfaces:** compare/score pages that got us in front of Motional in the first place.
- **Simulator scaffolding:** physics-based weld simulator with closest-match caching (the seed of the Goldak data factory).

This is the credibility anchor: **we already have a working sensor-to-PDF pipeline.** The pilot's "trojan horse" is a 1–2 week extension of code that exists, not a moonshot.

### The R&D roadmap (the moat)
We don't stop at feature-based XGBoost/GRU — those find correlations but **cannot model hidden state, can't predict fusion depth with physics grounding, and can't tell you when they're extrapolating.** Our moat is a **physics-informed world model** that reconstructs the one hidden quantity every defect descends from — the **thermal cycle** (peak T + t8/5 cooling). From that single state, five defects fall out as read-heads:

```
                    ┌─ fusion depth ──┬─ too shallow → LOF / LOP
   THERMAL CYCLE ───┤                 └─ too deep    → BURN-THROUGH
   (peak T, t8/5)   │
                    ├─ time above temper T → HAZ hardness ─┬─ too low  → HAZ SOFTENING
                    │                                      └─ too high → COLD / HYDROGEN CRACKING
                    └─ solidification gradient → HOT CRACKING
```

HAZ **softening** and HAZ **cold cracking** are *the same Myhr–Grong hardness number read at opposite tails* — one physics formula, two defects. That's a story no feature-based competitor can tell.

The build draws on 10 papers, each a named part of one machine: Goldak '84 (simulator), Raissi '19 (physics penalty), Chen '18 + Rubanova '19 (the clock + reader), LeJEPA '25 (label-free encoder), DreamerV3 '23 + PINN-welding '25 (stable training), PHOENIX '25 (read-heads), Settles '12 (active learning), IRIS '22 (later streaming version). **None of it is exotic — every piece has public reference code; our job is adapting it to 4 sensor channels.**

### Why this team
The rare four-way intersection: **embedded hardware + ML/physics-informed modelling + welding-metallurgy domain depth + maritime GTM access.** Most teams have one or two. We have working hardware *and* a credible physics roadmap *and* (via Marc) the domain/sales insight to sell into the most conservative buyer in the market.

### Honest line between shipped and research
- **Shipped & pilot-ready:** sensor rig, ingestion, 11 features, verdict system, PDF logs, the three demos.
- **Research (off the pilot's critical path):** the world model. Its headline claim — fusion-depth prediction — **can only ever be validated by cutting our own coupons open** (destructive testing), which a production-weld pilot can never do. So: the shipyard pilot proves the *cheap* model on their data; **our own sectioned coupons prove the moat.** Two separate validation tracks; neither substitutes for the other. We keep the world model behind the pilot so a research setback never sinks the commercial milestone.
